# Posolo privacy policy

Effective date: [effective date]

This policy describes the Posolo app published on Google Play.

## In short

Posolo keeps everything you enter on your phone. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and the app does not connect to the internet. The developer never receives your data. It leaves your phone only when you export it, back it up to a folder you choose, or share a summary yourself.

## What Posolo stores

Posolo stores what you enter and the settings you choose:

- Weigh-ins, waist measurements and day notes.
- Medicine doses: the date, time, strength, injection site, whether a dose was skipped, and a note; and which medicine you take, including any medicine you add yourself.
- Side effects, and your daily answers about appetite, sleep and mood.
- Non-scale victories: short notes about progress the scale does not show.
- Your profile: height, target weight, dose schedule and reminder settings.
- App settings: the app lock (your PIN is stored only as a salted hash, never as the PIN itself), how the estimated level is shown, when you confirmed the statement on the first screen, and the folder chosen for automatic backups.

## Where it is stored

Everything is kept in Posolo's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Posolo does not add encryption of its own; it relies on the encryption Android applies to the phone's storage. The app lock keeps Posolo's screens closed to anyone else using your phone, but it does not encrypt your data.

## What Posolo does not do

- No account: you never sign in or register.
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools inside the app.
- No advertising.
- No internet access: the app does not ask Android for permission to use the network, and it sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

If you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give the developer statistics about crashes and app performance (Android vitals). Google collects these under its own terms. They describe where the app went wrong, not what you entered.

## When your data leaves your phone

Only when you send it:

- Export saves a backup file (CSV) in a place you choose.
- Automatic backup, if you turn it on, saves the same file once a day to a folder you choose, and keeps the newest seven of these files.
- Summary for your doctor creates a PDF and hands it to an app you pick from Android's share sheet.

These files are not encrypted: anyone who can open them can read your log. If the place you choose belongs to a cloud storage service, for example a folder that a cloud storage app keeps in sync, the files are then held by that service under its own terms and privacy policy. Posolo itself never uploads them.

## Android's own backup

Posolo turns off Android's automatic backup to your Google account, so your log is not copied there. When you move to a new phone with a cable or a direct phone-to-phone transfer, Android may copy some of Posolo's internal settings, such as how the estimated level is shown, but not your log, your app lock, the statement you confirmed or your backup folder: Posolo excludes them. To take your log to a new phone, export a backup (or use the automatic backup) and restore it on the new phone.

## Reminders and permissions

Reminders are notifications that Posolo schedules on your phone; they do not name your medicine. Posolo asks for permission to show notifications only when a reminder is turned on, by you or by restoring a backup. If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, Android checks your fingerprint or face itself and tells Posolo only whether it matched; Posolo never receives biometric data.

## How long your data is kept

Until you delete it. Backup files and summaries you saved or shared stay where you put them until you delete them there. The automatic backup deletes its own files beyond the newest seven, and never any other file.

## Deleting your data

- In Profile, Delete all data removes everything Posolo stores on your phone. It also stops the automatic backup and forgets its folder, but leaves the files already in that folder.
- Uninstalling Posolo removes everything stored in the app.
- Backup files and summaries you saved elsewhere are deleted where you saved them.

## Your rights

The developer does not receive or hold any of your data, so there is nothing for the developer to send you, correct or delete. Export gives you a complete copy of your log in a common format (CSV) at any time. If you have a question or a concern about how Posolo handles data, contact the developer at the address below. You can also contact the data protection authority in your country; in Finland, that is the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

## Children

Posolo is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new effective date, and the app shows the new text after an update.
